# Apple HIG Guidelines
- Emphasize content-first design, translucent backgrounds (`backdrop-filter: blur`), and restrained color palettes.
- Maintain strict typographic hierarchy with tight tracking on headers and optimal body line-height.
- Smooth corner radiuses (`curve` aesthetics, consistent 12px–20px rounding on cards).
